adddd (ADD-driven development) is a plugin for coding agents, for brains that want exactly one thing in front of them. One ticket, one prompt, and it ships to a background worker in its own worktree.

It comes back only when it needs you; by then the next card is already up. Every card comes with numbered options, the ones most likely to be right, and you can always type your own instead.

This page works the same way: the sash stands in for your tracker, type /adddd and the plugin picks from it, or press 1 below to watch it drive itself.
